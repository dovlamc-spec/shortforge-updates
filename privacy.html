Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 (v2 - TikTok and license keys)

This Privacy Policy explains how ShortForge Studio ("the App", "we", "us"), a Windows desktop application made by Wadisha, collects, uses, stores and shares information, including information received from Google APIs and YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to let you upload videos you created in the App to your own YouTube channel. By connecting your YouTube channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is handled according to the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information we access from your Google account

Permission (scope)What we do with it
youtube.uploadUpload a video you created in the App to your channel, with the title, description, tags, privacy status and publish time you chose - only when you click "Upload", "Upload all ready", or turn on auto-upload.
youtube.readonlyRead only the name of your connected channel, so the App can show which channel is connected.
TikTok: user.info.basicShow the display name of your connected TikTok account.
TikTok: video.upload, video.publishSend a video you created in the App to your TikTok account (as a draft in your TikTok inbox, or posted directly if you choose) - only when you click "TikTok" or turn on automatic sending.
openid, email, profile (Google sign-in)Create and sign in to your ShortForge account. We store your email address and account ID.

We do not read, download, edit or delete your existing videos, comments, playlists, subscribers or analytics.

3. How your data is stored

  • On your computer: your settings, the API keys you enter, your list of topics and videos, and the YouTube access token. The YouTube token is encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) and can only be read by your Windows user account. This data never leaves your computer, except as described below.
  • On our account server (Google Firebase): your email address, account ID, account role, license status and expiry date, number of free trial videos used, and account creation date.
  • Error reports: only if you click "Send error report", the App sends the error message and the recent App log, your email, App version and basic settings (voice and render type) to our Firebase database so we can fix the problem. API keys are removed from the report before sending.

We do not store your YouTube access token, videos or channel data on our servers.

4. Third-party services

To make videos the App sends text or search terms directly from your computer to services you choose: AI text providers (for example Groq, Google Gemini, Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter) for writing scripts, voice providers (Microsoft Edge / Azure Speech, ElevenLabs) for narration, and Pixabay for stock footage and photos. Only the video topic, script text and search terms are sent. Each service is governed by its own privacy policy.

TikTok: your TikTok access token is stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows Data Protection. We do not read your TikTok videos, followers, messages or analytics. Disconnect at any time in Settings > TikTok, or in the TikTok app under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.

License keys: if you buy Premium, your license key is checked with our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad) to activate Premium. The provider receives the key and a computer name; payment details are handled only by the provider.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not transfer it to third parties except as necessary to provide the App's features you use, to comply with the law, or with your consent. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Humans do not read your Google user data, except with your explicit consent (for example when you send an error report), for security purposes, or to comply with the law.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Disconnect YouTube at any time in the App (Settings > YouTube > Disconnect). This deletes the token from your computer.
  • You can also revoke the App's access at the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Local data is deleted when you uninstall the App and remove the folder %APPDATA%\ShortForgeStudio.
  • To delete your ShortForge account and all data on our server, email us. We delete it within 30 days.

7. Data retention

Account data is kept while your account exists. Error reports are deleted after the problem is resolved, and at the latest after 12 months.

8. Security

Connections to our server and to Google use HTTPS. Access to account data is limited by database security rules.

9. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version is published on this page with a new date.
