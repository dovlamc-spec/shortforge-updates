Upload and schedule to YouTube

Optional. Connect your own channel with Google sign-in.

Connect your channel

In Settings > YouTube click "Connect YouTube" and sign in with Google. ShortForge asks only for permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read your channel name (youtube.readonly).

Pick your publish times

For example 12:00 and 18:00 every day. ShortForge finds the next free time slot for each video.

Upload

Click "Upload" on a video, "Upload all ready", or turn on auto-upload. Each video is uploaded to your channel with its title, description and hashtags, set to publish at the scheduled time.

ShortForge Studio only uploads videos that you created in the app, and only when you ask it to. It never reads, edits or deletes your other videos. You can disconnect at any time in the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See our Privacy Policy.
